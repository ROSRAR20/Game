# Privacy Policy for Swarm Gate

**Last updated:** November 17, 2026  
**Application:** Swarm Gate (`com.rosra.swarmgate`)  
**Developer:** Rosra Games  

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application **Swarm Gate** (the "Application"), developed and published as a portrait 3D arcade mob shooter game for Android.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Application operates as a self-contained, fully offline video game.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

- **No Personal Data Collected**: The Application does **not** collect, harvest, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address).
- **No Analytics or Telemetry**: The Application does **not** integrate third-party analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Unity Analytics).
- **No Third-Party Advertising**: The Application contains **zero** advertisements and integrates no ad networks or tracking SDKs.
- **No Online Accounts**: The Application does not require account creation, registration, or login of any kind.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

The Application stores gameplay progression data (such as level unlock progress, mission stars, and audio preferences) **locally on your device** using Android's native device storage (`SharedPreferences`).

- This data never leaves your device.
- It is not accessible to external servers or third parties.
- You can erase this data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings (`Settings > Apps > Swarm Gate > Storage > Clear Data`).

---

## 4. Permissions

The Application requests minimal standard hardware permissions strictly necessary for core offline arcade gameplay:
- **Haptic Vibration (`VIBRATE`)**: Used solely to provide tactile force feedback during cannon firing, gate passages, and explosive impacts.

The Application does **not** request permissions for camera, microphone, contacts, location, or phone state.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

The Application is suitable for audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is collected at all, the Application is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in this repository and accompanied by an updated effective date.
